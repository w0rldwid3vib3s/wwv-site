# Meridian

Practice IQ batteries with published weights. A free 12-item Quick Gauge, then five longer forms: balanced, culture-fair, verbal, quantitative, and spatial. Each form publishes its weights. You get an index, a range, and a plan for the weak spots.

**Play it:** https://valley-ruby-reef-brush.grok.me

Mira is the practice guide. She stays with you from the first item through the report.

## What this is not

Meridian is a practice battery. It is not an admissions test, not a clinical evaluation, and not a supervised sitting. The line at 130 is a reference mark on a mean of 100 and a standard deviation of 15. It does not qualify anyone for anything. Culture Form is original items in the spirit of a culture-fair matrix sitting. It is not Raven’s, not Cattell, and not a Mensa paper.

Scores stay on the device. This repository is the public product page (readme and cover). It does not include the item bank.
